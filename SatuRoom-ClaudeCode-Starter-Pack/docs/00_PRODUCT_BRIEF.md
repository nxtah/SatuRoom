# Product Brief

## One-sentence description
SatuRoom is a business software ecosystem intended to help businesses run daily operations through approachable web products tailored to each industry.

## Confirmed direction
- Brand/domain: SatuRoom; intended main marketing domain `saturoom.com`.
- First operational product: football academy web app, intended `bola.saturoom.com`.
- Marketing and operational apps are distinct product surfaces; independent deployments are preferred.
- Web is the current priority; native mobile is deferred.
- Owner has a VPS and domain, but provider/OS/DNS/TLS/server state must be audited.
- UX should be friendly, visual, clear, and low-clutter for nontechnical owners and staff.
- F&B and other industries may come later, but are not current scope.

## Product thesis (hypothesis, not validated research)
Small and medium businesses may find complex enterprise dashboards overwhelming. SatuRoom should reduce the effort to understand the system, set it up, and complete common tasks. Do not claim demand or product-market fit is proven.

## First product candidates (validate before final MVP)
Academy setup, staff invitations/roles, player records, teams/squads, training schedules, attendance, guardian relationships, billing, and player development. Prioritize based on actual workflows, not feature count.

## Provisional users
- Owner: configures academy, controls access, reviews business status.
- Manager/admin: handles daily operations.
- Head coach: oversees squads, schedules, attendance, and coaching staff.
- Coach: works with assigned teams/sessions.
- Parent/guardian: possible later access to their own child's information; MVP inclusion is undecided.

Do not assume children need their own logins. Parent-managed profiles may be simpler initially, but validate.

## Success measures to define after discovery
Time to setup, create first player/team, schedule training, complete attendance; task success; confusion/support incidents; and authorization test coverage. Do not invent numerical targets without baseline data.
