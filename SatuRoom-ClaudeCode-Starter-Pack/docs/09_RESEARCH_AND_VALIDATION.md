# Research and Validation Plan

## Evidence status
No interviews, observation, usability tests, or competitor validation are documented in this pack. Treat assumptions as hypotheses.

## Objective
Understand how academy owners/staff currently handle player registration, squad assignment, schedules, attendance, parent communication, billing, and reporting; identify the smallest high-value workflow.

## Suggested early sample
3–5 owners/managers and 2–4 coaches; optionally 2–3 parents if parent workflows are in scope. Qualitative discovery only, not statistically representative.

## Interview prompts
Ask participants to walk through their current process for registering players, assigning teams, communicating schedules, taking attendance, handling absences, deciding what information they need, and managing access. Ask what breaks, what is repeated across WhatsApp/spreadsheets/paper, what tools they tried, and what would make them distrust a new system. Ask to see the workflow where possible; avoid leading with the proposed solution.

## Usability tests
Give tasks rather than button instructions. Observe hesitation, mistakes, misunderstood labels, workarounds. Track completion, errors, confidence, and follow-up questions. Use fictional records early. Protect personal data.

## Output
Anonymized notes, patterns/counterexamples, severity-ranked issues, validated vs unvalidated assumptions, MVP recommendations, and decision log updates. Never fabricate participants, quotes, findings, or validation claims.
